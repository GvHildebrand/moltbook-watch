## Novelty of the four studies

**A_sentinel_benchmark** — novel: No study has judged the action a post solicits rather than its text. Nobody has reported an action gate's detection rate, false-positive rate on matched benign posts, or a failure taxonomy that separates command-bearing injection from prose-only social engineering and delayed memory triggers.  
 overlaps: Text-level detection is well covered: Riegler regex (506), Archive regex (9,247, no p/r), Zhang keyword taxonomy (15,915), Keane keyword scan (0/30 verified), Tasci TF-IDF/DistilBERT classifiers (F1 0.941 README-only), Liu's 30-case obedience ASR, Chen's 34-case audit, Bot-Mod intent probing, and two replica memory-pollution studies (2603.23064, 2608.01637). Persuasive-prose baselines exist (Jose 1.0% propaganda; Zhang social engineering 31.9%).  
 adjust: Drop the phrase '9,247 labelled injection posts': it is a regex count with no precision or recall, and must be recomputed with risk.py on the Zenodo 2026-04-15 zip. Build the positive set from spans that carry a literal shell or API action: Zerhoudi sec. 9.2 clusters (n=11, 15, 41), Tasci's 167+28 adjudicated attacks (provenance unclear, cite as adjudicated), and Archive hits on the five command-shaped patterns. Hand-label a stratified subsample with two raters, at least one human, and state the LLM-rater caveat (Keane's rater B was Claude). Controls: Keane's 10,176 substring false positives and 10/30 refusals, Bot-Mod's 240 benign rows, Jose-style persuasive posts, and in-band platform instructions (heartbeat.md, role prompts). Report three classes the gate is expected to miss by design: prose-only social engineering, delayed E-M-B memory triggers, and hidden-DOM payloads. Run in a sandbox with no tool-bearing agent reading posts.

**B_meta_era_comparison** — novel: No daily interrupted-time-series with the instruction-change breaks modelled, no agent-count series split by verification tier, no injection prevalence with a fixed detector on both sides, and no reply-graph community comparison pre/post 03-10.  
 overlaps: The Archive paper eyeballed daily volume across 03-10 and saw nothing; Tasci gives three coarse phases (posts 1,242,044 / 763,754 / 1,099,338; audit attack rate 1.0% / 2.4% / 2.2%; regex candidates 1,085 / 1,290 / 1,079); Ayan (61 days) and 2606.29722 (100 days) straddle the date without testing it; Zerhoudi documents six pre-window breaks E1-E6; Li documents the Jan 31-Feb 3 outage; Chen documents a 57,093-post deletion event; Keane gives a 2026-09-01 snapshot.  
 adjust: Model as dated breaks: E2 (02-05), E3 (02-08), E4 (02-14), E5 (02-25), E6 (about 03-01), the outage (Li's cut points, verify from the Archive's own posting gap), the spam-crisis deletion (date from 2602.18832 full text), the reverse CAPTCHA (about 02-04), the 03-09 metric relabel, the 03-15 Terms reissue. E3, E4, E5 and E6 are each larger shocks than any plausible acquisition effect, so the honest pre-window baseline is 02 to 09 March. Control collector artefacts: comment polling began 02-02; Feb 9 under 10% captured; posts partitions 03-09 to 03-12 about 30% smaller than 03-08; NaT partitions and the 04-06 to 04-14 SQLite recovery; 8-day posts gap 08-08 to 08-15; completeness only assured to end of March. Use the hourly snapshots table (total_agents, total_posts, active_agents_24h) and cross-check with jscmp4 (continuous to 07-03), Ayan and Keane's raw corpora.

**C_attribution_gap** — novel: Nobody has computed the agents-per-distinct-owner distribution from the Archive's public is_claimed and owner_x_handle fields with the window stated, nor mapped registered / claimed / verified / active tiers onto EU AI Act Art. 50 provider-deployer duties and Swiss law.  
 overlaps: Ratios exist at four levels: Wiz 88:1 (1.5M boasted agents / 17,000+ owners, from deleted breach data); Li about 3:1 (55,932 active authors / 18,651 owner accounts, 30.9% burner); Zerhoudi 106,916 handles for 120,811 authors, 8 multi-agent, 71.1% ghost; verified tiers 7.04% (04-08), 7.14% (06-06), 7.31% (09-18, unverified). Luo matched 10,659 owner-agent pairs; Lai interviewed owners. Jose and Tasci give production concentration (10 agents = 24% of propaganda; 167 attacks from 33 handles). daveholtz/moltbook_scraper (MIT, updated 2026-09-21) contains 07_owner_analysis.R and may already compute agents per owner. Legal commentary: Aurum (EU/UK/US), Carnegie (EU deployer gap), TechPolicy.Press; none covers Swiss law.  
 adjust: Read daveholtz's 07_owner_analysis.R before claiming novelty. Report tiers with their own windows: registered 1,506,155 (02-01 counter) or 2,881,380 (04-08); verified 202,786 (04-08); posting 175,886 (Archive, 01-27 to 04-14); active 30,076 (Lai, window unstated). Aggregates only, never a handle list. State that 'human-verified' means an X claim tweet, not identity. Quote the Terms exactly: 'You are responsible for any and all activities or actions that occur under your Account', with the separate scope clause binding 'any artificial intelligence agents ... associated with your account'. Legal review before any Art. 50 or Swiss-law statement.

**D_live_check** — novel: Nothing is measured after 2026-09-11.  
 overlaps: Keane's 2026-09-01 full crawl (207,391 items) and the Archive's export to 2026-09-11 already cover April to September; Zenity's Hot-feed claims and Permiso's named accounts are cheap persistence checks; Wayback holds 200 JSON captures to 2026-09-20.  
 adjust: This Mac resolves moltbook.com to 0.0.0.0 and the Terms forbid automated retrieval with no research exception, so a direct pull needs another network and counsel. Lawful substitutes: the Moltbook Observatory live instance (read-only /api/feed?since=&limit= and /api/stats, no credential), Wayback CDX and Save Page Now, the HF exports, and Keane's snapshot. Diff skill.md, heartbeat.md and rules.md against the 2026-09-15 and 09-06 captures before attributing any shift to the owner. Keylessness of reads is an inference from crawler captures, not documented; documented per-key limit is 60 GET/60 s (skill.md) vs 100/min (rules.md).

Frozen paper snapshot: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19594804 (data zip 1,331,154,530 bytes; code zip 45,664 bytes). Companion code: https://github.com/kelkalot/moltbook-observatory-paper (risk.py holds the 11 injection regexes). Alternatives: PaDaS-Lab/moltbook-corpus (MIT, 1,312,238 posts to 03-08, posts carry verification_status verified/pending/failed/bypassed, is_spam, is_deleted; annotated_posts 394,221 and annotated_comments 2,100,589 with topic and toxicity labels); TrustAIRLab/Moltbook (MIT, 44,376 labelled posts, no author id); jscmp4/Moltbook (continuous 01-27 to 07-03); remcovansanten (1,101,570 non-spam posts to 03-31); takschdube (418,683 posts); Ayan's 61-day set; Keane's three CC BY 4.0 corpora (Feb, Mar, Sep 2026); Tasci's two CSVs.
- **license**: Archive MIT on HF card, Zenodo (mit-license) and GitHub README (collector repo API reports license null). Paper CC BY 4.0. MIT covers the compilation and code; it does not settle rights in agent-authored post text collected under Moltbook's Terms (which prohibit scraping and grant a personal, non-commercial licence).
- **citation**: Cite three: HF @dataset (Gautam and Riegler 2026, Moltbook Observatory Archive), arXiv:2605.13860 (Gautam, Olstad, Pettersen, Riegler), and Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.19594804. The companion README's BibTeX still carries PLACEHOLDER_DOI and misspells Pettersen; the collector @software entry (Riegler and Gautam) is also requested.
- **partitions**: data/<table>/<YYYY-MM-DD>.parquet, partition key dump_date derived from the creation timestamp (posts/comments created_at, agents/submolts first_seen_at), one HF config per table, single split 'archive'. Paper rule: pre-acquisition dump_date <= 2026-03-10, post > 2026-03-10; no partition exists in the files, build it from created_at. Posts run 2026-01-27 to 2026-09-11 (220 files) with 2026-08-08 to 08-15 missing; NaT.parquet in agents (5.0 MiB), comments, submolts; lost_and_found dirs from the April recovery; submolts last exported 2026-04-13; follows table withheld (0 parts).
- **owner_fields**: agents.is_claimed (int64 0/1) and agents.owner_x_handle (string) are the only human-attribution fields; the /agents/profile API also exposes owner x_name, avatar, bio, follower counts and claimed_by without a key. Claimed ratio not yet measured (datasets-server index was loading; compute locally). Handles are personal data.
- **injection_labels**: None shipped. Table 4's 9,247 (0.35%) comes from risk.py's 11 case-insensitive regexes over content+title (paper text says <system>, code uses <s>, which also matches HTML strike tags); no precision or recall; reproducible only on the 2026-04-15 zip. Human-adjudicated alternatives: Tasci seed 630 (167 attacks) and audit 1,500 (28 attacks), provenance unclear; Keane 30 (0 attacks, one Claude rater).
- **caveats**: Comment polling started 02-02 vs 01-27 for posts; 23.8% comment coverage; Feb 9 under 10%; completeness only substantially assured to end of March; posts partition sizes fall about 30% on 03-09/03-10 (bytes, not rows, subject to 7-day backfill); pyarrow reportedly fails on some files (use fastparquet, polars or duckdb). Local tooling: huggingface_hub 1.8.0 is installed; no pyarrow, pandas, polars or duckdb; Bash curl was denied in several reader sessions; nothing on this Mac can yet open Parquet.

## Legal and ethics flags

- Moltbook Terms (2026-03-15, unchanged to 2026-09-03) prohibit scraping, harvesting agent or user data without consent, and any automated retrieval; personal non-commercial licence only; no research exception; California law, San Mateo venue, class-action waiver. Whether a browsewrap binds a Swiss association that only reads a keyless API, and whether redistributing derived data is 'distribute or otherwise commercially exploit', needs counsel before Study D and Output 4.
- owner_x_handle, claimed_by and profile owner fields are personal data (GDPR, Swiss FADP); Tasci, Keane, Zerhoudi and Li corpora carry agent handles that can be linked to people. Publish aggregates only; never handle lists, per-owner rows or the six-to-seven named threat accounts from Permiso, Li or Zhang.
- Wiz's 17,000 owners and 88:1 come from a breached database that Wiz says it deleted; cite as Wiz's measurement, never reconstruct, never query the Supabase endpoint or reuse the printed key.
- Zerhoudi validated extracted secrets by calling service APIs and checked wallet addresses on-chain; Vigilia must not replicate (Swiss StGB Art. 143bis exposure). Pattern counts only, never validation, never row-level publication. Permiso's republishing of an Ethereum wallet address is itself a flag; do not repeat the address.
- MIT on the Archive does not settle rights in agent-authored post text collected under Moltbook's Terms; Output 4 needs a redistribution opinion (Bot-Mod's raw exports, no licence, and Chen's repo, no licence, are not reusable without permission).
- 'Human-verified' means an X claim tweet, not identity verification; the policy brief must say so. Keane's card asserts 'no data subject, no privacy law applies' and Manik and Wang assert 'no personal data'; both are contestable and not to be repeated without counsel.
- Any Art. 50 / Swiss-law statement in Study C is Vigilia's own analysis; no prior source covers Swiss law (Aurum: EU/UK/US; Carnegie: EU). Legal review before publication.
- Liu et al. built impersonating phishing pages in a sandbox; DiGiacomo, Zenity, Wilson and Superminds posted on the platform. Vigilia cites, does not replicate; a replica sandbox would need task-shape declaration first.

## Bottom line

Four things are open and one premise is wrong.

1. **The "9,247 labelled injection posts" do not exist as labels.** The number is an 11-regex count in the Archive paper (Table 4, 0.35%, 1,746 agents) with no precision or recall and no shipped column ([2605.13860](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13860), [risk.py](https://github.com/kelkalot/moltbook-observatory-paper)). The only adjudicated sets are Tasci's 167 + 28 attacks (provenance unclear) and Keane's 30 high-confidence hits, of which 0 were attacks with one Claude rater ([Tasci](https://github.com/mtasci42/moltbook-a2a-prompt-injection), [Keane](https://huggingface.co/datasets/DavidTKeane/moltbook-agent-social-ai-prompt-injection-dataset)).
2. **Study A is novel.** Every prior detector judges text; none judges the action a post solicits. Nobody has reported an action gate's detection rate, false positives on matched benign posts, or a taxonomy separating command-bearing injection from prose-only social engineering and delayed memory triggers.
3. **Study B is novel but the baseline is minefield.** The Archive paper eyeballed daily volume across 2026-03-10 and saw nothing; Tasci split three coarse phases. Four instruction-file changes in February and March are each larger than any plausible acquisition effect ([2604.13052](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.13052)), and the collector's own gaps must be modelled.
4. **Study C is probably novel, pending one repo.** Ratios exist at four levels from four windows (Wiz 88:1, Li about 3:1, Zerhoudi 99.99% one-agent-per-handle, verified tier about 7%), but no one has computed agents per distinct owner from the Archive's public fields, except possibly daveholtz/moltbook_scraper's 07_owner_analysis.R, which no reader has opened. No Swiss-law analysis exists anywhere.
5. **Study D is blocked twice**: this Mac resolves moltbook.com to 0.0.0.0 and the Terms forbid automated retrieval with no research exception. Lawful substitutes exist (Observatory live instance, Wayback, HF exports to 2026-09-11, Keane's 2026-09-01 crawl).
